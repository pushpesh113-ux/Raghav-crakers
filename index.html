Burst crackers safely

  • Light crackers only in an open area, away from houses, vehicles and dry leaves.
  • Keep a bucket of water nearby, and children always with an adult.
  • Light one at a time, and never relight one that did not go off. Pour water on it.
  • Wear cotton clothes, and keep loose dupattas and sarees away from the fire.
  • Follow your city’s rules on which crackers are allowed and when you can burst them.
